About

Christina McCarthy

I am a Mindfulness Meditation Teacher (MMTCP 2023), a 200-hour Hatha Yoga instructor, and a Yin Yoga teacher, currently expanding my studies through the FLUID Yoga program grounded in Tibetan Buddhism.

My work is shaped by training in Trauma-Sensitive Mindfulness and by the wisdom of teachers.

Nourished by life in the green mountains, I feel most at home in nature, silence, and community. A former professional pastry chef and a grateful mother of three, I bring curiosity, compassion, and humor to the path of practice and to those I’m fortunate to walk it with.
