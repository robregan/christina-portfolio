About ✨

Christina McCarthy

Christina McCarthy is a mindfulness meditation teacher, yoga instructor, and lifelong student of practices that nourish body, mind, and spirit.

Her path began in a different kind of practice: the kitchen. A 1999 graduate of the New England Culinary Institute, Christina trained professionally as a chef before discovering her calling in baking. 🧈

After a decade away from professional kitchens, her relationship with nourishment has taken a new form through meditation, yoga, and Ayurveda.

Christina graduated from the Mindfulness Meditation Teacher Certification Program in 2023, studying with Tara Brach, Jack Kornfield, and the Greater Good Science Center at UC Berkeley. 🪷

She practices Vipassana meditation and continues to deepen her studies in Buddhist teachings, yoga, and trauma-sensitive mindfulness.

Christina is currently immersed in a 220-hour Ayurvedic training, exploring Ayurveda as another expression of something that has shaped her life from the beginning: the art of nourishment.

Her approach is grounded in compassion, curiosity, humor, and respect for the teachers and traditions that came before her. She believes that working compassionately with others helps us stay connected to life — and that we all respond to gentle nourishment and reminders to love. 🩷
